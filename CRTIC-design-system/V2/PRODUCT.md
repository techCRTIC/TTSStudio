## Register
brand

## Users
Four audience tiers (dossier §5.2), addressed in two modes:
- **A) Audiencias** (público general) and **B) Beneficiarios** — tuteo ("Tú"), 18–35, close and
  friendly. This is the default voice ("Modo CRTIC").
- **C) Clientes / partners** and **D) Alianzas / Co-Ejecutores** — "Ud.", formal but sharp
  ("Modo Centro Tecnológico").
The job to be done: understand what CRTIC can create with technology + creativity, see real proof,
and start a conversation (hire us, join us, cover us).

## Product Purpose
CRTIC (Centro para la Revolución Tecnológica en Industrias Creativas) is a Chilean R&D+i center
that fuses technology and creativity — "tecnocreatividad" — to move a new creative industry.
Surfaces built with this system (sites, decks, banners, app UI) exist to make that capability
legible and desirable, and to convert the four audiences. Success = it feels premium, unmistakably
CRTIC, and never generic.

## Brand Personality
Archetype: **the Magician** — transformation, vision, creativity, guidance, courage. Voice is
optimistic and clear; we open from possibility, never from dry definition. Tuteo by default.
Three-word personality: **visionary, clear, transformative.** Tagline: **"El Futuro sí existe."**
Signature line: **"Creatividad que mueve la nueva industria."** Always speak in positive; never
sound lastimero. Coin neologisms when useful, but stay understandable.

## Anti-references
What CRTIC must NOT look like:
- AI-purple / blue-glow "AI startup" aesthetic. No.
- Glassmorphism-**as-default**, gradient text, neon, uniform heavy drop shadows on every card. (A
  single purposeful veil over a live backdrop, and one machined/focal card per view, are earned — not
  this cliché.)
- SaaS-cream / beige-brass "premium consumer" cliché.
- The 2023 navy + orange "dark-tech" look (retired for the web — see §5.8).
- Generic 3-equal-feature-card grids, hero-metric templates, fake dashboards.
- Em dashes anywhere in copy. Scroll cues, section-number eyebrows, decorative status dots.
- Anything a viewer could guess from the category alone ("creative tech = dark + neon").

## Design Principles
1. **Apple-sober restraint.** Snow white + soft black, vast negative space, calm hierarchy. The
   surface has a point of view through confidence, not noise.
2. **One orange, earned.** `#FA4515` is the only accent — CTA, hover, at most one highlight per
   view. Monochrome everywhere else. **Depth is earned the same way:** machined/focal shadow and the
   accent glow belong to one focal surface per view, never everywhere.
3. **Motion must be motivated.** Every animation justifies itself (hierarchy, feedback, story).
   transform/opacity only; custom easings (the **wave** ease-in-out-sine for lifts/glides so surfaces
   breathe, not snap); respect reduced motion.
4. **Show the real work.** Prefer real footage/cases over decoration. Proof over adjectives —
   **and the product can prove itself:** a live, interactive data field (the sign-in backdrop) makes
   "tecnocreatividad" legible in the surface itself, not in a claim. The dashboard is the hero.
5. **Clarity for the audience.** If the Magician casts a spell, the audience still understands it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Soft-black on snow ≈ 15:1. Secondary grey only on large text. The single
orange accent doubles as the focus ring (≥3:1). `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere
(reduce = gentler: keep opacity/color, drop movement and stagger). Real `<a>`/`<button>`, visible
focus, keyboard-navigable; pinned/scroll-hijack views always ship a native-scroll fallback.
